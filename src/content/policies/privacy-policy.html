YANA Chocolateria ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit yanachocolateria.com or order from us, how we use it, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, mobile number, the items you order, your chosen pickup branch or delivery address, and any order notes.
  • Payment status: whether your payment went through and its reference number. Payments are handled by our payment provider, HitPay. We never see or store your card number, e-wallet PIN or bank login.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, phone or social media.

Your shopping cart is saved in your own browser so it's still there when you come back. It isn't sent to us until you check out. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • to prepare your order and arrange pickup or delivery
  • to send your order confirmation and contact you about your order
  • to handle refunds, replacements and other concerns
  • to keep the business records required by law, such as for tax and accounting

We don't sell your information, and we don't send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed, with service providers that help us run the shop:

  • HitPay, to process your payment
  • couriers such as Lalamove or Grab, who receive your name, mobile number and delivery address for courier orders
  • our website, database and email providers, which store and send order information on our behalf; some of their servers may be outside the Philippines

We may also disclose information when required by law or by a lawful order of a government authority.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill your order and handle any concerns, and for as long as tax, accounting and other laws require. After that, we securely delete or anonymize them.

How we protect it

Our website uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections, our order records are accessible only to authorized staff, and our service providers are required to protect your information.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to have it corrected, to object to its processing, to have it erased or blocked, to receive a copy of it, and to claim damages. To use any of these rights, contact us below. If you believe we've mishandled your data, you can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Children

Our website isn't intended for children under 18. Please have a parent or guardian place orders.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its date at the top.
