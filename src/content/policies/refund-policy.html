Every YANA chocolate is handcrafted in small batches from Philippine cacao. Because our products are perishable food, we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind. But if something isn't right with your order, we'll make it right.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please check your order as soon as you receive it. If an item arrives damaged, defective, spoiled, incomplete, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 24 hours of pickup or delivery with:

  • your order number (it starts with "YC-")
  • clear photos of the item and its packaging
  • a short description of the problem

Please keep the item and its packaging until we've resolved your concern. Once we've confirmed the issue, we'll offer you a replacement (for pickup at our branch, or redelivered) or a full refund for the affected items, whichever you prefer.

Courier deliveries

For courier orders, we pack every order to protect it in transit. If your order is damaged on the way, contact us within 24 hours with photos and we'll work it out with you and the courier. Chocolate is sensitive to heat, so please refrigerate or store it in a cool, dry place (ideally 16–20°C) as soon as it arrives. We can't replace items that melted or bloomed after delivery because of how they were stored.

Cancellations

  • You can cancel for a full refund until we start preparing your order. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number.
  • Once we've started preparing your order, or for custom and special orders, we can't accept cancellations.

Pickup orders

We'll let you know when your order is ready. Please collect it from the branch you chose within 3 days. Orders not collected within 7 days of being ready are forfeited and can't be refunded, as we can't keep perishable items fresh beyond that.

If we can't fulfill your order

If an item becomes unavailable after you've paid, we'll contact you to offer a replacement item or a full refund for that item.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method (GCash, Maya, card, or bank) through our payment provider, HitPay. We process refunds within 5 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or e-wallet, it may take a few more days for the refund to appear. Courier fees paid directly to the courier are not refundable through us.
